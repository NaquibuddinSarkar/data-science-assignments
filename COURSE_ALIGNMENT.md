## Notes on authorship and evidence

- The original course archive was curated into a separate 61-file ZIP package. Those exact files are not yet imported into this repository.
- The supplementary notebooks and assignment briefs were authored as additional practice based on the course topics.
- Synthetic examples are labelled and must not be cited as empirical results.
- The FastAPI deployment demo's CI workflow has run successfully in GitHub Actions; this is not equivalent to validating every notebook in a clean runtime.
- The PV degradation and waste forecasting notebook is a methodological practice case, not an estimate of national PV waste and not a result from an academic thesis.
